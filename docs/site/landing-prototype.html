How Medra works

Find a doctor, pay once, keep the record.

  1. 1

    Find a verified doctor

    Every practitioner is checked against the MDCN register before they can take a booking. Search by specialty, location or availability.

  2. 2

    Book and pay in the app

    See the fee before you agree to it. Pay once, get a confirmation you can show at any desk, and reschedule without a phone call.

  3. 3

    Your record follows you

    Notes, results and prescriptions land in one record you control. Share it with the next doctor for exactly as long as you choose.
