How Medra works

Verify once. Then book, treat and record on one file.

  1. 1

    Everyone is verified once

    Members prove who they are with their NIN. Doctors are checked against the MDCN register by a person, not a script. Hospitals, clinics and labs register with their CAC certificate and practice licence.

  2. 2

    Book, treat, record — on the same episode

    The member sees real availability and pays before leaving home. The doctor opens a prepared file, not a blank one. The lab, pharmacy and front desk enter their own work instead of the doctor typing it.

  3. 3

    The record follows the member

    Notes, results and prescriptions land in one record the member controls, and travel to the next doctor or hospital for exactly as long as consent lasts.
