<h1 align="center">Medra</h1>

**A unified medical records & consultation-booking platform for Nigeria.**

Medra replaces paper-based medical filing and WhatsApp booking with a single system for
patient records, institutional data, staff communication, and consultation booking. Patients
find verified doctors/institutions, see real availability, book (in-person or virtual), get SMS
reminders, and carry a portable medical history. Institutions pay a subscription; patients use
the platform free and pay institutions directly for services.

- **Launch market:** Abuja (pilot: 3–5 private clinics)
- **Platform:** mobile-responsive web (MVP)
- **Business model:** institution/practitioner subscription (priced by size); free for patients

## MVP in one paragraph

Medra MVP is a mobile-responsive web app where a patient can register with their phone number,
search for a verified doctor in Abuja, see their real availability, book an appointment (in-person
or virtual), receive an SMS confirmation and reminders, and view the consultation note the doctor
writes after the visit. The doctor gets a dashboard showing today's schedule, can confirm or
decline bookings, mark appointments complete, and write a consultation note for each visit. The
clinic pays a monthly subscription after a 30-day free trial.
